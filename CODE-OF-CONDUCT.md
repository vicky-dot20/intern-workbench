# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive learning and contribution environment. Be respectful, specific, patient, and focused on the work. Do not demean beginners or use review authority to intimidate. Private information and reports must be handled discreetly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or retaliation. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or ban participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact method configured by the adopting organization; do not expose a reporter publicly.
