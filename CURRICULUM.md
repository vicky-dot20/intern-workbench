## Progression

- Level 0 — Absolute Beginner: ORI through early HTML; can navigate, follow setup, and submit a guided PR.
- Level 1 — Guided Learner: HTML through JS; can implement scoped requirements with procedural guidance.
- Level 2 — Independent Implementer: TS through TEST; can plan, build, validate, and explain a feature.
- Level 3 — Contributor: DEBUG through SYSTEM; can investigate, review, refactor, and reason about systems.
- Level 4 — Junior Developer Ready: REAL and FINAL; can clarify product work and deliver it through multiple reviewed PRs.

Progress is established by the [mentor rubric](docs/mentor/EVALUATION-RUBRIC.md), not task count alone.
